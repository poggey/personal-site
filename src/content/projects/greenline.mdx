## The question

{/* DRAFT */}
What does a one-person safety consultancy need from its website? Credibility, not lead generation.

## What I built

{/* DRAFT */}
A custom site for Greenline HSE, an Australian health, safety and environment consultancy. Six sections, and a booking and enquiry flow built from scratch that feeds a private dashboard for the owner.

## How it works

{/* DRAFT */}
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with ten restrained GSAP animations and full reduced-motion support. The logo was upscaled with OpenCV to 7190 × 2490 pixels.

## What it found

{/* DRAFT */}
The brief was credibility, not lead generation. The booking flow is custom, with no Calendly or Acuity.
